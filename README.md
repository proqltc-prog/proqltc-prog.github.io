# proqltc-prog.github.io

Root of the developer website that the Google Play listings of proqltc apps point to (https://proqltc-prog.github.io).

- `app-ads.txt`: authorised ad sellers, required by AdMob to verify the apps (Jira ZR-101). Keep the line exactly as AdMob shows it under Apps > app-ads.txt.

The game site itself lives at https://proqltc-prog.github.io/seed-and-destroy/ (repository proqltc-prog/seed-and-destroy).
